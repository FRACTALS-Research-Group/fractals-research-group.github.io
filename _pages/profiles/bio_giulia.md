<p>
<b>Assistant Professor in Biostatistics</b> at <i>University of Milano-Bicocca</i> and Principal Investigator of a <b>“My First AIRC Grant 2025” (MFAG)</b>, a prestigious five-year project funded with €500,000. Her research focuses on <b>advancing thyroid cancer diagnosis through Artificial Intelligence</b>, integrating <i>medical imaging, molecular biology, and clinical data</i> to improve diagnostic accuracy and reduce unnecessary surgical interventions.
</p>

<p>
Her work centers on the development of <b>AI-driven multi-omics integration frameworks</b>, combining <i>digital pathology images, MALDI mass spectrometry imaging, and clinical datasets</i>. These systems are designed to automatically identify <b>cellular patterns and molecular biomarkers</b>, providing <b>interpretable risk predictions</b> to support clinicians in complex diagnostic scenarios, particularly in indeterminate thyroid nodules.
</p>

<p>
She obtained her <b>PhD in Biostatistics and Clinical Research</b> from <i>University of Milano-Bicocca</i> in 2019 with excellent results, following a degree in <b>Mathematics</b> from <i>University of Milano</i> (2016). Her doctoral research focused on <b>MALDI-imaging proteomics</b> for the identification of biomarkers in thyroid biopsies for clinical diagnosis.
</p>

<p>
She is a member of the <b>Bicocca Bioinformatics, Biostatistics, and Bioimaging research group</b> and collaborates with the <b>Mass Spectrometry group</b> at <i>University of Milano-Bicocca</i> and <i>San Gerardo Hospital (Monza)</i>.
</p>

<p>
Her research activity focuses on <b>innovative statistical methods for biomarker discovery</b> in biomedical research, with the goal of translating results from basic science into <b>clinically applicable tools</b> for precision medicine.
</p>

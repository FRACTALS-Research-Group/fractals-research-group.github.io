<h4><b>Francesca Dal Mas</b></h4>
<h6><i>Associate Professor of Strategic Management Accounting @ Ca' Foscari University of Venice</i></h6>

<p>
<b>Associate Professor of Strategic Management Accounting</b> at <i>Ca' Foscari University of Venice</i>. She holds a <b>bachelor's and a master's degree in Business Administration</b> from the <i>University of Udine</i>, a <b>bachelor's degree in Law</b> from the <i>University of Bologna</i>, and a <b>PhD in Managerial and Actuarial Sciences</b> from the <i>Universities of Udine and Trieste</i>.
</p>

<p>
Before joining <i>Ca' Foscari University of Venice</i>, she was a <b>Senior Lecturer in Strategy and Enterprise</b> at the <i>University of Lincoln</i>, United Kingdom. Since <b>January 2024</b>, she has also been part of the <b>clinical research team at SAN University in Lodz</b>, Poland.
</p>

<p>
Her research interests include <b>healthcare management, intellectual capital, knowledge management, sustainability, digital transformation, and new business models</b>. Her research explores how organizations can leverage knowledge, digital technologies, and innovative approaches to address contemporary managerial and sustainability challenges, particularly in healthcare and other knowledge-intensive contexts.
</p>

<p>
She is a member of <b>Ipazia, the international observatory on gender research</b>, and of <b>Ca' Foscari's Sustainability Lab</b>. As of <b>July 2026</b>, she is also a member of the <b>IEEE Computational Intelligence Society Bioinformatics and Bioengineering Technical Committee</b>.
</p>

<p>
She holds the <b>national accreditation as a full professor in accounting (Secs-P07)</b> and as an <b>associate professor in organization science (Secs-P10)</b>. She has also been recognized among the <b>top 2% of scientists according to Stanford University's ranking</b>.
</p>

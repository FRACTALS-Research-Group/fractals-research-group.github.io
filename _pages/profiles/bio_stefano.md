Stefano Della Torre is a permanent <b>Senior Researcher (Primo Ricercatore)</b> at <i>INFN Milano-Bicocca</i>. He received his <b>PhD in Astronomy and Astrophysics</b> from the <i>University of Insubria</i> in 2012, with a thesis on Monte Carlo stochastic simulation of cosmic-ray propagation in the heliosphere.

He is the main developer of <b>HelMod</b>, a reference model for the solar modulation of galactic cosmic rays (electrons, antiprotons and nuclei up to Z=28, over the full 22-year solar cycle), and leads its GPU successor, <b>COSMICA</b>. Within the <i>GALPROP-HelMod</i> framework (INFN and University of Milano-Bicocca, INFN Bologna, Stanford), he contributed to the most accurate local interstellar spectra of cosmic-ray species up to Z=28.

He served as deputy work-package leader for HPC code enabling and optimization in Spoke 3 of the <i>ICSC National Centre for HPC, Big Data and Quantum Computing</i> (2022–2025). In 2024–2025 he was scientific coordinator of the ICSC cascade-funding project <b>SDEGnO</b>, carried out with <i>Ca' Foscari University of Venice</i>, on the search for GPU parameters for COSMICA. He currently coordinates <b>NEXUS-GPU</b>, a sub-project of the ICSC Grants project <i>FR2IA – From fundamental research to industrial applications</i>.

A member of the <b>AMS-02</b> collaboration since 2009, he co-coordinated the AMS Low Energy analysis group (2014–2018), which measured proton, helium, electron and positron fluxes for each Bartels rotation. He led the space-radiation work package of the <i>ASI-INFN-ENEA ASIF</i> programme (2022–2025), developing the public HelMod online calculators and cosmic-ray forecasts available at [helmod.org](https://www.helmod.org). In <b>LiteBIRD</b>, he coordinates the study of cosmic-ray systematic effects, including a machine-learning case study on HPC.

He was an invited co-author of the ISSI review on galactic cosmic rays throughout the heliosphere (<i>Space Science Reviews</i>, 2022), serves as a reviewer for ISCRA (HPC allocations) and PRIN, and has co-authored <b>69 journal publications</b>.

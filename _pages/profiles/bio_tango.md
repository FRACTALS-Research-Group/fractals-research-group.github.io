Andrea Tangherloni is an <b>Assistant Professor</b> in the <i>Department of Computing Sciences at Bocconi University</i>, where he joined in December 2022. He earned his <b>BSc, MSc, and PhD in Computer Science</b> from the <i>University of Milano-Bicocca</i> in 2013, 2015, and 2019, respectively, with doctoral research on <b>high-performance computing for complex problems in the life sciences</b>.

Before joining Bocconi, he was a <b>Research Associate</b> in the <i>Department of Haematology at the University of Cambridge</i> and at the <i>Cambridge Stem Cell Institute</i>, with visiting research at the <i>Wellcome Sanger Institute</i>. He subsequently held <b>postdoctoral positions</b> at the <i>University of Bergamo</i> and the <i>Bocconi Institute for Data Science and Analytics (BIDSA)</i>.

His research lies at the intersection of <b>artificial intelligence, computational biology, and bioinformatics</b>, combining <b>deep learning and high-performance computing</b> to model <b>single-cell omics data</b> for personalised and regenerative medicine.

He teaches on the <b>MSc in AI</b> and the <b>MSc in Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence in Health Sciences</b> at Bocconi University. He also leads <b>DECODE (FIS-2024-02465)</b>, a project developing <b>single-cell RNA and ATAC foundation models</b> for <b>gene regulatory network inference</b>.

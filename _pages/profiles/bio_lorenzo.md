Lorenzo Cobianchi, MD, PhD, is an <b>Associate Professor of General Surgery</b> at the <i>University of Pavia</i> and a senior consultant at the <i>IRCCS San Matteo University Hospital</i>, where he leads the <b>Pancreas Unit</b>.

He directs the <b>residency program in General Surgery</b> at the University of Pavia and co-leads the Italian committee of program directors working to shape the future of surgical education.

His research focuses on <b>new technologies in surgery</b> and <b>surgical education</b>, in both elective and emergency settings. He is a member of the board of the <i>World Society of Emergency Surgery (WSES)</i> and, since January 2024, part of the clinical research team at <i>SAN University</i> in Łódź, Poland.

He is included in Stanford University's <b>World's Top 2% Scientists</b> ranking.
